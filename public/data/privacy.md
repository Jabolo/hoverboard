GDG DevFest Warsaw 2026 is a community-led event organized by Google Developer Groups Warsaw. This page explains the data handled directly through this event website. For questions or a request to remove newsletter data, contact [contact@gdgwarsaw.pl](mailto:contact@gdgwarsaw.pl).

## Newsletter updates

When you choose to subscribe, we collect your email address and, depending on the form you use, your first and last name. We use this information to send relevant GDG DevFest Warsaw updates.

Subscription records are handled through this website’s event infrastructure, which may include Firebase and Mailchimp when newsletter delivery is configured. You can ask the organizers to remove your subscription data at any time. If an event email includes an unsubscribe link, you can use that link too.

## Registration through Evenea

Ticket registration is provided in an embedded Evenea form. Information entered in that form is handled by Evenea under its own terms and privacy information. Review the information shown in Evenea before submitting your registration.

## Community standards

DevFest is a community-led developer event hosted by Google Developer Groups. Our [Code of Conduct](/coc) applies to this event. We also follow the relevant Google Developer community guidance, including the [Google community and event resources](https://developers.google.com/community) and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Google’s policies apply to Google services. This notice covers the event website and the direct newsletter workflow described above; it does not replace the privacy notices of third-party services such as Evenea.
