## What is GDG DevFest Warsaw 2026?

GDG DevFest Warsaw 2026 is a community-led technology conference organized by GDG Warsaw.

## When and where is it happening?

The event is planned for **November 21, 2026**, at Google for Startups Campus Warsaw, Plac Konesera 10, 03-736 Warszawa.

## Who is the event for?

Developers, designers, engineers, researchers, students, entrepreneurs, and anyone interested in learning and sharing around technology.

## When will tickets be available?

The ticketing flow is being prepared in Evenea. Ticket types, pricing, and the registration opening date will be announced before the event is published.

## When will the programme and speakers be announced?

The programme and speaker lineup will be added as they are confirmed. Subscribe to event updates to hear when new information is available.

## How can I speak at DevFest Warsaw 2026?

The Call for Papers will be announced separately. The website will link to the Advocu submission form once the final URL is confirmed.

## How can my company support the event?

Use the **Become a partner** contact form on this website or email [contact@gdgwarsaw.pl](mailto:contact@gdgwarsaw.pl). Sponsorship details are shared directly with interested partners.

## Where can I ask another question?

Contact the organizing team at [contact@gdgwarsaw.pl](mailto:contact@gdgwarsaw.pl).
